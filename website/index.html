How it's private.

Most chat apps run through a company's computers. Sanctum has none.

No company in the middle

When you write to a friend, your phone hands the message straight to theirs. There's nowhere else for it to be stored, read or sold.

Nobody sees who you talk to

Everything travels through Tor, so nobody watching can tell who is talking to whom, and not even your friends learn where you are.

No number, no account

You add a friend by scanning their code in person, or with a short code they send you. That's it.

Locked on your phone

Chats are stored encrypted, behind your fingerprint, face or PIN. No cloud copies, no backups.

+ =

Check a chat at a glance

Everyone gets a sigil, artwork made from their secret key. Your chat with Joe has its own sigil, made from both of you, and it looks the same on both phones. Hold them side by side: if the sigils match, nobody is listening in.

Honest about the trade-offs

  • Android only. iPhones don't let apps stay connected in the background without a company's server.
  • Private takes a moment. Tor makes video calls choppier and Reels slower. Two switches, Faster calls and Faster reels, speed them up and say plainly what they share.
  • No backups. If you lose your phone, its chats are gone too.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, and no data to sell. The code is public, so anyone can check what it does.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

Each version is built from the public code and signed with Sanctum's own key. Android only installs updates signed with that same key, so a tampered copy can't replace yours.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. iPhones stop apps from staying connected in the background, and the usual way around that needs a company's server, which Sanctum doesn't have.

What if my friend is offline?

Your phone keeps the message and delivers it when you're both online. In groups, other members pass it on, so it reaches people even after the sender has gone offline.

Can anyone read my messages?

No. They're locked on your phone before they leave it, and only your friends' phones can open them. Sanctum's developer can't read them either.

How many people can I chat with?

Sanctum is made for close friends: a circle of up to about 200 people, and groups of up to 50.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Its chats are gone, because there are no backups. Your friends keep their side. Install Sanctum on your new phone and add them again.
